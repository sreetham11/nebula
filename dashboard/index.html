Schematic side view, not to scale. The silhouette is for orientation; the chip grid under each car is the readout. Unit chips (Door, Bogie) are coloured by that unit's fused risk level. System chips (A/C, Light, Batt, Radio, Traction, Brake) are coloured by how far that individual sensor channel sits from the healthy fleet mean, signed so that a brake capacity 4σ below healthy and a bearing temperature 4σ above it both read as severe.
